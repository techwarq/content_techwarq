Ch. 05Questions

Before you ask.

How fast is delivery?

24–72 hours depending on the plan, from when we have your brief and assets. Full month clients get priority 24–48h turnaround.

Are you a professional studio?

No, and that's the point. I'm a solo creator who learned to direct AI tools well. No agency overhead means founders get lots of good content at a price that fits an early-stage budget.

What do you need from me?

Just your website or product link. For clips, a link to the long video. For reels, a topic or script (or I write it). I ask a few quick questions if anything's unclear.

Do you use AI? Is it "AI slop"?

Yes, I use AI, openly. It writes first drafts, voices, finds footage and speeds up the edit. That's why it's fast and cheap. But I'm the one telling the story: I pick every clip, fix the pacing and cut anything that looks off before you see it. If it feels like slop, you don't pay.

Can I use my own voice or footage?

Yes. Send a voice note or clips and I'll cut to them word by word. Otherwise I use a natural AI voice and licensed-style footage.

What if I don't like it?

You get one free revision on every video. If it's still not right on your first order, you don't pay.

How do I pay?

Pay after you've seen your first draft. For Full month, monthly by card or UPI/PayPal — cancel anytime.
